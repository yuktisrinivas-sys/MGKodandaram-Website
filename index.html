About

M. G. Kodandaram is a retired Indian Revenue Service (IRS) officer who served as Assistant Director at the National Academy of Customs, Indirect Taxes & Narcotics (NACIN), Bengaluru, from 1981 to 2018 — a distinguished career spanning nearly four decades in service to the nation.

Today, he continues to serve as an Advocate and Consultant, and is recognized as a Master Trainer of GST for the Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs (CBIC), Government of India. He has been a guest resource person and faculty at more than 80 training institutes and academies, training thousands of officers in taxation and related laws.

With over 405 articles published in various journals and magazines, he has conducted more than 3,126 training sessions (since 2012) for trade bodies, government authorities, and tax practitioners. He is also a Founder Member of the Foundation of Data Protection Professionals in India (FDPPI).
